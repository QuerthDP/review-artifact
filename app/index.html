Moving around

[ ]
Previous / next commit.
j l
Move the cursor up / down one line, hunk header, file header or GitHub reviewer thread.
↑ ↓
Same as j / l; Up on the first line moves into the commit stepper, where Left / Right change commit and Down comes back to the diff.
PgUp PgDn
Scroll one screen, keeping two lines of overlap, and put the cursor on the line at the same height.
n p
Jump to the next / previous comment on the page (proposed, queued, reviewers', conversations with Claude and proposed changes); a GitHub reviewer thread also takes the cursor.
← →
In the split view, put the cursor on the old (left) or new (right) version of the line, so k and i comment on or ask about that version; the side stays as you move. From the new side → moves into the queue panel, and ← comes back to the diff. In the queue, Up / Down pick an item and Enter jumps to it.
c
Jump to the commit message at the top; press again to go back to where you were.
v
Mark the file holding the cursor as viewed, or unmark it. A viewed file collapses and the cursor moves to its header; unmarking expands it again. On the commit stepper (reached with ↑, or a commit button with focus after a click or Tab, or right after [ or ] with no line selected) it marks every file of that commit as viewed, or unmarks them all; a commit whose files are all viewed shows as viewed there.
h
Show or hide the queue panel.

Commenting

k
Write your own review comment on the selected line (or range), or your own reply on the selected reviewer thread. It is queued as yours and mirrored into your pending GitHub review.
Shift+j l / ↑ ↓
Select a range of lines in one file; k, i and m then act on the whole range. Shift+click on a line button does the same.
Enter
On a line: comment on it. On a hunk header: expand the hidden lines. On a file header: collapse / expand the file. On a card: reply, continue the conversation, or tell Claude how to revise its comment.
q
Queue the proposed Claude comment on the selected line or card.
d
Dismiss (or restore) what is selected: reject a proposed comment, close a conversation with Claude, hide or show a change applied at your request, or resolve or reopen a reviewer thread (in the local app on GitHub too; elsewhere on this page only).
Enter q d on a change
On a proposed change card: Enter or q accepts it (on your own PR Claude folds it into the commit and pushes the review branch to your fork, otherwise it is queued as a GitHub suggestion); d declines it, or restores a declined one.
r
Toggle "Reviewed by Human" on the selected Claude comment, after you have read its exact text.
u
Open the submit panel. Up / Down pick Comment, Approve or Request changes; Enter arms the button, Enter again submits.

Asking Claude

i
Ask Claude about the selected line or range; continues the conversation already open there. On a selected comment it talks to Claude inside that comment: a Claude comment opens "Ask Claude to revise", a conversation its follow-up box, and your own comments and reviewers' threads (commit-level ones too) a conversation drawn inside them. The answer stays on the page.
m
Ask Claude to change the code there: Claude proposes the change as a card to Accept or Decline.
a
On a selected reviewer thread (or the PR conversation: its latest comment), ask Claude to draft a reply; say what it should say or just press Enter. Claude proposes the reply as a card under the comment, to queue, revise or reject.
f
Open the feedback panel to report a problem with this tool. It opens in the text box; Esc leaves the box, then j / l and Enter move through the panel (including the resolved-feedback toggle), and Esc again closes it.

Text boxes and panels

Enter
In a text box: send.
Shift+Enter
In a text box: new line.
Esc
Leave the text box and close open panels, composers and selections (unsent text is kept as a draft).
t
Show or hide this list. While it is open, j / l or Up / Down step through it, PgUp / PgDn / Home / End scroll it, and t or Esc closes it and returns to where you were.
